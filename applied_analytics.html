Selected pathway Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Bootcamp
Applied Data Analytics and Business Intelligence Training

Transform organisational data into strategic business decisions

Every organisation generates substantial volumes of data across finance, sales, customers, operations, marketing, supply chains and human resources. Competitive advantage depends on the ability to convert that information into reliable insight that improves performance, controls costs, identifies opportunities and strengthens executive decision-making.

The SI Analytics Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Bootcamp develops professionals who can move confidently from raw business data to decision-ready analysis. Participants work with authentic datasets similar to those used by multinational corporations, consulting firms, banks, technology companies, government agencies and international organisations.

What you will gain

  • Understand how finance, sales, customer, marketing, workforce and operational data support business decisions.
  • Import, organise, clean, validate and combine business datasets using Microsoft Excel and R.
  • Define decision-relevant KPIs and analyse performance, trends, variance, growth and operational efficiency.
  • Apply descriptive and inferential methods to practical finance, customer, workforce and market questions.
  • Build interactive Power BI dashboards using Power Query, data modelling, DAX and time-intelligence measures.
  • Translate analytical findings into concise executive narratives, recommendations and decision-ready reports.
  • Complete an end-to-end business intelligence capstone suitable for a professional portfolio.
  • Receive an SI Analytics Certificate of Completion and continued access to learning resources.

Applied Healthcare Analytics Training

Transform healthcare data into actionable intelligence

Healthcare organisations generate enormous volumes of data through electronic medical records, insurance claims, health surveys, disease surveillance systems, clinical research and routine facility reporting. Many professionals, however, have never been formally trained to convert these datasets into reliable evidence for programmes, research, clinical services and health-system decision-making.

The SI Analytics Healthcare Data Analytics Bootcamp bridges that gap through authentic datasets similar to those used by ministries of health, CDC, WHO, USAID-supported programmes, hospitals, NGOs, pharmaceutical organisations and research institutions.

What you will gain

  • Understand the healthcare data ecosystem and the structure of electronic medical records, claims, surveys, surveillance, clinical research and facility data.
  • Import, organise, clean, validate and manage healthcare datasets using Microsoft Excel and R.
  • Apply descriptive and inferential methods to practical clinical, programme and population-health questions.
  • Create clear visualisations and interactive Power BI dashboards for healthcare monitoring and decision support.
  • Build reproducible analytical workflows in R and communicate findings through decision-focused reports and presentations.
  • Interpret healthcare indicators, trends, variation and disparities with appropriate analytical context.
  • Complete an end-to-end healthcare analytics capstone suitable for a professional portfolio.
  • Receive an SI Analytics Certificate of Completion and continued access to learning resources.

Curriculum for Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Bootcamp
Six-Week Curriculum

Data Analytics and Business Intelligence Learning Pathway

A progressive, applied curriculum that moves from business-data foundations to analysis, interactive reporting and an executive-ready capstone project.

  1. 1Foundations of Business Data Analytics. The business data ecosystem; analytical thinking and decision questions; introduction to Excel, R and RStudio; importing data; variables and data types; reproducible workflows; working with finance, sales, customer and operations data.
  2. 2Business Data Preparation and Management. Data-quality assessment; missing values, duplicates and inconsistent categories; cleaning and transforming data in Excel and R; reshaping, joining and validating datasets; preparing reliable analytical tables.
  3. 3Business Performance and Statistical Analysis. Descriptive statistics, KPIs, variance, growth and trend analysis; profitability and sales performance; customer and market summaries; segmentation, correlation and practical statistical interpretation.
  4. 4Customer, Workforce and Operational Analytics. Customer behaviour, satisfaction and retention; survey and opinion-poll analysis; workforce metrics; supply-chain and operational efficiency; translating analytical findings into business recommendations.
  5. 5Power BI for Business Intelligence. Power Query, data modelling and relationships; DAX measures and time intelligence; KPI cards, filters, drill-through and interactive dashboards; designing clear reports for managers and executives.
  6. 6Capstone Project and Executive Presentation. Complete an end-to-end business intelligence solution using an authentic dataset; integrate data preparation, analysis, visualisation and interpretation; present a polished dashboard with strategic recommendations.

Software, datasets and applied learning experiences include:

Six-Week Curriculum

Healthcare Data Analytics Learning Pathway

A progressive, applied curriculum that moves from healthcare data foundations to reproducible analysis, interactive reporting and a decision-ready capstone project.

  1. 1Foundations of Healthcare Data Analytics. Introduction to healthcare analytics and the healthcare data ecosystem; types of healthcare data; understanding claims data; introduction to R and the RStudio workflow; data import; healthcare variables and data types; reproducible analysis.
  2. 2Healthcare Data Management in Excel and R. Importing EMR, claims, survey, surveillance and facility data; data-quality assessment; missing data; recoding and validation; cleaning, reshaping and joining datasets; building transparent analytical workflows.
  3. 3Descriptive Epidemiology and Statistical Analysis. Frequencies, proportions, rates and summary measures; stratified analysis; cross-tabulation; trends and variation; confidence intervals; selecting and interpreting statistical methods for healthcare questions.
  4. 4Healthcare Data Visualisation and Evidence Communication. Visualising health indicators in R; selecting appropriate charts; communicating trends, comparisons and disparities; producing reproducible analytical reports and decision-focused narratives.
  5. 5Power BI for Healthcare Intelligence. Power Query, data modelling, relationships and DAX measures; building healthcare KPIs, filters, maps and interactive dashboards; designing clear views for programme and executive decision-making.
  6. 6Capstone Project and Decision-Ready Presentation. Complete an end-to-end analysis using an authentic healthcare dataset; integrate data preparation, analysis, visualisation and interpretation; present a polished dashboard or analytical report with actionable recommendations.

Software, datasets and applied learning experiences include:

  • Microsoft Excel

  • Power BI

  • Insurance Claims

  • Disease Surveillance
